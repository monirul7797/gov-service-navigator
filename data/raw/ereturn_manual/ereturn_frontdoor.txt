etaxnbr.gov.bd — e-Return portal landing page

The e-Return portal presents six service tiles on its landing page, under the National Board of Revenue, Bangladesh header:
- eTIN — Register as a taxpayer
- eReturn — File your tax return / Download Express Certificate
- ReturnVerify/PSR — Verify your Return
- eReturnLedger — Update your tax payment
- eTaxPayment (eTDS) — Manage your source tax
- eTaxService — Interact online with tax support team

This is the entry point a first-time visitor sees before registering or signing in — distinct from the post-registration "Return submission" flow (Assessment / Income / Rebate / Expenditure / Asset & liabilities / Tax & payment / Return view) already documented elsewhere in the corpus.

nbr.gov.bd — parent NBR homepage

The main National Board of Revenue homepage is the umbrella site above etaxnbr.gov.bd; it covers NBR's three broad domains (NBR general, VAT, Customs) alongside Income Tax and eTIN/eReturn as one of several quick-link tiles (others: Baggage Rules, VAT Registration/VAT Return Filing).

Top navigation: About Us, eServices, Regulations, Form, Tax Types, Budget, Project, Publications, EFDMS, FAQ (plus a language toggle to বাংলা).

Contact numbers displayed prominently in the header: 16555 (VAT), 333 (general government helpline, not tax-specific), 16139/BSW, and 09643717171 (labeled "e-Return Hotline Number" — matches the number already documented in the e-Return FAQ's support-contact record). A separate "কল সেন্টার" (call center) promotional box elsewhere on the page lists a second number, ০৯৬১২২৮২৪২৪ — not previously documented, a distinct contact channel from the main e-Return hotline.

Other homepage features: a 13-digit BIN (Business Identification Number) search tool for looking up basic taxpayer information; a Latest Updates / Press Notes / News & Media tabbed panel listing recent NBR notices (postings, transfers, retirement orders — internal administrative notices, not citizen-facing); sections for About Us, National Integrity Strategy, Annual Performance Agreement, Right to Information, ICT Corner, Grievance Redress System, and Citizens Charter.
