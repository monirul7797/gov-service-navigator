SECTION: Correction of NID Information (জাতীয় পরিচয়পত্র সংশোধন)
SOURCE: nidw.gov.bd / services.nidw.gov.bd

GENERAL RULES:
In case of correction of information, the applicant must apply online with supporting documents. In all cases, the SSC certificate will get strict priority if the person is SSC qualified.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS FOR NID INFO (Name, DOB, Parents' Name):
- SSC / HSC Certificate (Highest priority).
- Online Birth Registration Certificate.
- Passport.
- Driving License.
- Magistrate Court Affidavit.
- Service Book (for government employees).
- Newspaper published circulation (for radical name changes).

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS FOR OTHER INFO (Address, Marital Status, Blood Group):
- Marriage Certificate (Kabin Nama) or Divorce Certificate (Talaknama) / Court Decree for marital status changes.
- Sibling's NID copies (helpful for verifying parents' names).
- Utility bills, house rent, or holding tax receipts for address correction.
