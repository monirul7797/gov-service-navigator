SECTION: New Voter Registration (নতুন ভোটার নিবন্ধন)
SOURCE: nidw.gov.bd / services.nidw.gov.bd

UPDATING PROCESS:
Updating of the voter list is an ongoing process. Data collection and integration take place in two phases: household visits for form completion, followed by a visit to the registration center for photographs, fingerprints, and data entry.

QUALIFICATIONS (Article 122 of the Constitution & Section 7 of Electoral Rolls Act 2009):
- Must be a citizen of Bangladesh.
- Must not be less than 18 years of age (Citizens aged 10 and above can register for a biometric NID, but will only be included in the voter list upon turning 18 on or before January 1st of the current year).
- Must not stand declared by a competent court to be of unsound mind.
- Must be a resident of the electoral area.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (For Form-2 Enrollment):
1. S.S.C. or equivalent certificate (Proof of age).
2. Birth Registration certificate (Proof of age).
3. Passport / Driving license / TIN certificate (Proof of age).
4. Utility bill copy / House rent receipt / Holding tax receipt (Proof of residence in that area).
5. Citizenship certificate (as applicable).
6. Father, Mother, Husband/Wife's NID card photocopy (as applicable).

PROCESS:
Fill out the form online at services.nidw.gov.bd, print the completed form, and contact the respective Upazila/Thana Election Office according to your schedule to provide biometrics (fingerprints, iris scan, photograph).
