SECTION: Issuance of Duplicate NID / Re-Issue (হারানো বা নষ্ট এনআইডি রিইস্যু)
SOURCE: nidw.gov.bd / services.nidw.gov.bd

RULES FOR LOST OR DAMAGED CARDS:
If a National Identity Card of any citizen is lost or damaged in any way, he/she may apply in a prescribed manner to the NID Wing for a fresh one online.

PROCESS & REQUIREMENTS:
1. A General Diary (GD) must be filed at the local police station immediately if the card is lost. The GD copy must clearly mention the NID number or Voter number.
2. The applicant must select "Lost/Damaged" on the online Reissue application form.
3. A scanned copy of the Police GD must be attached to the application.
4. Upon receiving and approving the application, the NID Wing will issue a fresh National Identity Card. 
5. Users will be notified via SMS and can download the temporary NID (online copy) from the portal or collect the smart card from the election office.
