SECTION: NID Wallet Application (এনআইডি ওয়ালেট)
SOURCE: Bangladesh Election Commission / NID Wallet App Guidelines

PURPOSE:
NID Wallet is a multi-purpose application developed by the Bangladesh Election Commission for Identity Verification. It adds a layer of security to ensure no one else can claim your account on the Bangladesh Election Commission website.

HOW TO USE:
1. Install the NID Wallet app on a smartphone with a camera and internet connectivity.
2. Disable the desktop mode of the browser while registering on the portal.
3. When the QR code page appears on the portal, launch the App by pressing the red button to scan the QR code.
4. Scan your face using the app to verify your identity.
5. If the scanner fails, the browser provides a code that can be copied and entered into the app manually.
