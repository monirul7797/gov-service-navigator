Process rule encoded by this form (not verbatim portal text — derived, kept because the five-step page omits it):
The office that will handle an application is fixed before the application starts. The applicant first declares whether they are applying from inside Bangladesh or from abroad; that fork routes the application either to a Regional Passport Office or to a Bangladesh mission overseas. Inside Bangladesh, the applicant selects the District (জেলা) of their PRESENT address, then the police station (থানা) nearest to that present address, and the portal names the single Regional Passport Office where biometric enrolment must take place. Assignment is by present address — not permanent address, and not applicant choice.
