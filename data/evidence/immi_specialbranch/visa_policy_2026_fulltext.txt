32Mandatory visa conditions
1. The Ministry of Home Affairs will formulate policy relating to visa and immigration.
The Department of Immigration and Passports (DIP) will be the authority for issuing
visa including No Visa Required (NVR) to travel to Bangladesh, Visa on Arrival (VoA),
transit visa, exit pass, route change permit, and change of visa category in Bangladesh.
Bangladesh Missions will be the visa issuing authority in abroad. A visa is to be issued
upon considering individual application on case-to-case basis after careful examination
of documents and on being fully satisfied about the purpose of the visit and keeping
records in writing. Visa issuing authority may refer an issue to the Ministry of Home
Affairs for any decision, if necessary. Original Passports of visa applicants are to be
submitted to visa office while taking visa stamp.
2. A visa is also granted on the basis of relevant notifications and circulars as well as the
invitation from the government of Bangladesh. In cases of bilateral Agreements or
Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with other countries, visa will be issued
according to the agreements/MoU. Principle of reciprocity is to be followed for issues
that are not covered in visa policy and agreement/MoU.
3. For A2 categories of visa, United Nations and its organizations mean WHO, UNDP,
FAO, UNFPA, ILO, UNICEF, UNESCO, IMF, World Bank, WFP, IDB, UNCTAD,
UNHCR, IOM, UNODC, IAEA, UNIDO etc. International/Multilateral and Regional
organization and bilateral development agencies mean USAID, JICA, KOICA, JBIC,
DFID, CIDA, SIDA, DANIDA, PEACE CORPS, NORAD, FINNIDA, ICDDRB,
ADB, RED CROSS/RED CRESCENT, BIMSTEC, SAARC, COMMONWEALTH,
IFAD, OIC, IRENA, OPCW, ICC, PCA, EU, AFD, SDC, D-8 and other organizations
of similar type.
4. Machine Readable Visa (MRV) must be issued in all cases. In case of any emergency
situation different method can be prescribed by the Ministry of Home Affairs. When
computer program based/ electronically issued visa (e-visa) system will be introduced,
visa number has to be mentioned according to program code.
5. Visa issuing authorities have to mention following conditions on the visa label while
issuing visas:
a) For categories of A1, FA2, B, C, FE1, FE2, FE3, FJ, M, FM, FN1, N2, FP, FPI, R,
S, FSR, T, FBN, MT, MA, and RT with “Employment in Bangladesh prohibited’’.
b) At the time of issuing E1, E2, E3, J, P, PI and N1 category visa, passport of a person
arriving with a view to take up employment /work must be stamped with the
condition ‘please apply for work permit to BIDA/BEPZA/BEZA/BHTPA/ NGO
Affairs Bureau/PPA or other competent authorities within 15 (fifteen) working days
of first entry into Bangladesh.
336. A person arriving from India and Pakistan with a visa for 90 (Ninety) days or more has
to register with the immigration authorities at the port of entry within 14 (fourteen) days
of arrival.
7. Foreign nationals arriving from countries other than India and Pakistan will be exempted
from such requirement specified in condition no 6.
8. Application for an extension of visa has to be made 15 days before the expiry of the
visa. If application is lodged after the expiry date, penalty and visa regularization fee
(country fee) shall be applicable on the ground of overstay.
9.(a) The illegal stay of foreign nationals coming to Bangladesh on valid visas may be
regularized subject to the following penalty:
(i) For the first 15 (fifteen) days of illegal stay, at the rate of 1,000/-(one thousand)
Bangladeshi Taka per day;
(ii) If the period of illegal stay exceeds 15 (fifteen) days, at the rate of 2,000/-(two
thousand) taka per day up to a maximum of 90 (ninety) days; and;
(iii) If the period of illegal stay exceeds 90 (ninety) days, a penalty of 3,000/- (three
thousand) taka per day may be realized. At the same time, a case may be filed against
the foreign national concerned in the relevant court under the Foreigners Act (Act
No. 31 of 1946) due to illegal stay. The Additional Inspector General of Police,
Special Branch may take necessary measures to file a case under this order.
(iv) The fine for illegal stay of foreign citizens of Bangladeshi origin/foreign spouses and
children of Bangladeshi citizens may be waived.
(v) The fine for illegal stay of foreign students studying may be waived for up to 3 (three)
months. However, if the illegal stay exceeds 3 (three) months, a maximum fine of
Tk. 3,000 (Taka three thousand) may be realized. If the illegal stay exceeds 6 (six)
months condition 9 (a) (iii) will be applied.
(b) If it is necessary to consider the issue of waiver of fine imposed for illegal stay, the
Department of Immigration and Passports may send a proposal with its opinion to the
Ministry of Home Affairs in the following cases:
(i) Physical illness: If the stay is illegal due to physical illness, the Department of
Immigration and Passports shall duly examine the application submitted for waiver
of the said fine on the basis of a medical certificate issued by the concerned Civil
Surgeon or Professor/Associate Professor and send it to the Ministry of Home
Affairs along with appropriate recommendations. However, if the illegal stay due to
illness exceeds 5 (five) days, a certificate from the hospital authority stating that the
person is/was admitted to the hospital must be submitted.
34(ii) Other reasons: If the stay is illegal due to any other reasonable/credible/well-
grounded reason, the said department may send an application for waiver of the fine
payable along with a clear and reasonable grounds and detailed explanation along
with appropriate recommendations to the government/Ministry for consideration.
(iii) The above recommendations must be sent to Ministry of Home Affairs under the
signature of the Director General/ Additional Director General/ Director of the
Department of Immigration and Passports.
(c) For overstay of up to 7 (seven) days, the concerned passport holder can leave
Bangladesh by appearing at the Department of Immigration and Passports/Immigration
Authority at the airport/land port and paying the applicable fine, service charge and visa
fee. However, cash cannot be accepted for fine, service charge and visa fee. The
concerned passport holder can be allowed to leave Bangladesh if the said amount is
deposited in the prescribed code in a scheduled bank of the Government of Bangladesh
and the deposit receipt is presented. The period of illegal stay and the amount of fine
must be mentioned in the passport.
(d) If someone stays illegally due to unavoidable reasons such as natural disasters,
accidents, flight cancellations etc., the period of his stay will not be considered illegal.
10. The Ministry of Home Affairs may consider a note verbale, particularly on humanitarian
grounds, when deciding on an overstay fine for a foreign national. While overstay fines
are generally realized according to established rules, the MoHA has the authority to
consider mitigating circumstances, and a note verbale from the relevant Embassy/ High
Commission could be a relevant factor in such a consideration.
11. A visa category cannot be changed after entering Bangladesh, except for a Visa on
Arrival (VoA). A VoA may be converted only to the following categories: A1, D, ND,
A2, FA2, E1, E2, E3, FE1, FE2, FE3, J, PI, FPI, P, RT, FBN and NVR. A visa
conversion fee of US$200 is applicable only to those who undertake employment. UN
passport holders are exempt from the visa conversion fee. Applicants who enter
Bangladesh using their national passport shall also be exempt from the visa conversion
fee in cases of conversion to the A1, D, ND, A2 and FA2 visa category. For conversion
to the E2, E3, J, P, and PI categories, a recommendation letter addressed to the Ministry
of Home Affairs must be submitted from BIDA, BEZA, BEPZA, BHTPA, PPA or other
competent authority.
3512. Particular Nationals will get Visa on Arrival for 30 days (single entry) at the designated
port of entry while travel to Bangladesh for some particular reasons given below. The
visa issuing/immigration authority will grant VoA after being satisfied on examining the
invitation and relevant documents. (Such as hotel reservation, return ticket and proof of
finance in card or cash).
a) Investors/Development partners travelling to Bangladesh at the invitation of the
Government or investment authority;
b) Foreigners travelling to Bangladesh from those countries where there is no
diplomatic mission of Bangladesh;
c) Nationals of Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, China, Japan, South Korea, Brunei,
Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Egypt, USA, Canada, Brazil,
Australia, New Zealand, Russia, Turkey, UK and other European countries
travelling to Bangladesh for the purpose of tourism and business;
d) Foreign citizen of Bangladeshi origin and their spouse and children;
e) Officials of diplomatic mission in Bangladesh and the UN organizations;
f) Diplomatic and UN passport holders;
g) Foreigners with prior approval from Ministry of Home Affairs;
h) Transit Visa can be issued up to next connecting flight but not more than 72 hours
with charge of 20 USD or equivalent foreign currency;
i) The visa issuing/immigration authority will have to keep records regarding issuance
of VoA and submit monthly Statements to the Ministry of Home Affairs and DIP.
13. All visitors must hold a passport valid for 6 months.
14. Valid visa of an old passport can be transferred to a new passport upon application with
relevant country fees if applicable.
15. Visa fees will be applicable on the basis of reciprocity or agreement/arrangement/
memorandum of understanding. A, A1, A2, FA2, D and ND Visa will be gratis.
16. In case a report is required for issuance or extension of a visa, the concerned agency
(SB/ NSI) shall provide the report within 21 (twenty-one) working days of receiving
request from the visa authority. If no report is received within the time specified, visa
issuing authority may take appropriate decision taking into consideration that the said
agency has no objection on granting or extending the visa. No such opinion from the
agency shall be required for a child under 15 (fifteen) years of age.
3617. For the civil and military officials from overseas arriving for specific work and training
in the defense service institutions in Bangladesh, the recommendations of Armed Forces
Division (AFD) will be accepted instead of the work permit and security clearance.
Bangladesh Missions may grant multiple entry visa to those persons and their
dependents for additional 1 (one) month along with the full tenure of training with the
recommendations from Armed Forces Division.
18. If any foreign national arriving with the objective of employment or investment or
business or education purposes, conceals any information or violates any condition of
the visa issued, the appointing /sponsoring organization of that person shall be fined TK
1,00,000/- (one lac) per person for the first time and TK 2,00,000/- (Two lac) for similar
and any subsequent violation.
19. Student visa shall be granted to a foreign student willing to study at the educational
institutions which are approved by the Government of Bangladesh.
20. If a citizen of any country other than a visa prohibited country desires to visit
Bangladesh for tourism from a third country, Bangladesh Mission in that particular
country after being satisfied, may grant ‘T’ category visa in favor of the applicant.
21. Visa fees shall not be refunded if a visa application is declined.
22. A person arriving from a country which has been declared as public health emergency
of international concern by WHO may be required to undergo health screening or
additional measures as decided by the competent authority at the designated entry port
from time to time.
23. A2, E1, E2, E3, S, R, PI, FBN visas may be issued with maximum duration for the
respective category.
24. For other categories, visa can be granted for any duration up to the maximum approved
period for that specific category considering individual requirements.
25. For granting or extension of visa, Security Clearance means clearance issued by the
Ministry of Home Affairs. Police report will imply the report from Special Branch
of police.
26. With regard to the conversion and extension of a visa more than the maximum period
allowed in the circular, the Ministry of Home Affairs may waive, impose or relax any
condition mentioned in the policy.
3727. While shooting film/documentary, the foreign journalist must be accompanied by
designated representative of the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. And he will
have to submit ‘No objection certificate/clearance from the Ministry of Information and
Broadcasting’ at the immigration point before leaving the country.
28. NVR can be issued in foreign passports of Bangladeshi origin who have acquired
citizenship of any country in the world and foreign citizens of Bangladeshi origin of the
second and third generation and their spouse /children and foreign spouse/children of
Bangladeshi citizens and foreign investors to facilitate their entry and stay in
Bangladesh under the terms and conditions:
(1) In the case of Bangladeshi origin who have acquired citizenship of any country in the
world, except SAARC countries, and foreign citizens of Bangladeshi origin of the
second and third generation and their spouse/children
(a) Dual Citizenship Certificate or Bangladeshi MRP/e-Passport/National Identity
Card/Online Birth Registration Certificate/Citizenship Certificate of father/
mother/husband/wife;
(b) Certificate of Naturalization or legal documents from which the foreign citizenship
has been obtained;
(c) Affidavit given by father/mother/husband/wife in favor of the applicant regarding
the child/husband/wife;
(d) Marriage certificate (if applicable).
(e) Birth certificate (if applicable).
(2) In the case of foreign spouse and children of Bangladeshi citizens:
(a) Bangladeshi MRP/e-Passport/National Identity Card/Online Birth Registration
Certificate/Citizenship Certificate of husband/wife/father/mother;
(b) Affidavit of relationship given by father/mother/husband/wife in favor of the
foreign child/husband/wife;
(c) Marriage certificate (Nikahnama).
(d) Birth certificate (for children of Bangladeshi citizens).
(e) Police verification is required for the spouse and for children above 15(fifteen) years.
(3) The NVR facility will remain valid as long as the passport is valid. NVR application
fee will be 80 (eighty) US dollars or equivalent.
38(4) The NVR shall be issued after proper verification of the certificates submitted with
the application. If the applicant has NVR in his old foreign passport, NVR shall be
issued in the new foreign passport subject to payment of fees. In case of transfer of
NVR to the new passport of the foreign spouse of a citizen of Bangladeshi origin an
affidavit by his/her spouse will have to be submitted along with the application.
(5) Foreign Investors will get NVR facility for investment of USD 100,000 in heavy
industry or long-term business in Bangladesh. In that case, a certificate issued by
BIDA/BEPZA/BEZA/Investment Authority will have to be submitted along with
the application.
(6) In case of issuing NVR in favor of Foreigner of Bangladeshi origin and their
spouse/children who have obtained foreign citizenship or permanent residency
based on investment, a favorable report from the Bangladesh Financial Intelligence
Unit (BFIU) along with the investigating agency will be mandatory.
(7) Machine-readable NVR must be issued in the foreign passport.
29. Dependent family members mean spouse, parents, children, adopted children of
principal visa holder/applicant.
30. Development Partners: “Development Partners” means countries, international
organizations, development agencies, and other recognized institutions that work in
partnership with the Government of Bangladesh to promote sustainable development
and improve the well-being of the people of Bangladesh through financial, technical,
institutional, humanitarian, capacity-building, or other forms of development
cooperation.”
31 Validity of a security clearance provided by the Ministry of Home Affairs will be a
maximum of 5(five) years.
32. If any question is raised regarding visa as well as NVR which is not mentioned in this
Visa Policy clearly the Ministry of Home Affairs reserves the right to take any decision
including explanation/clarification and cancellation of Visa.
33. (1) The circulars issued previously concerning visa, are hereby repealed:
(2) Upon the repeal of the circulars, all visas issued previously will remain valid until expiry.
34. The Government reserves the right to cancel, modify, amend, or revise any part of this
circular or the visa policy at any time, if necessary.
35. This Visa Policy-2026 will come into effect immediately.
